Turn a photo into text

Take a photo of a printed page. Lectorist extracts the text so you can read it and tap words for help.

Your vocabulary

The words you look up become your vocabulary list.

Lectorist saves the words you translate, so your list grows from stories and texts you chose to read. When a word turns up again and you read it without looking it up, Lectorist brings it into your reading review so you can check whether you remembered it.

How one word becomes familiar

lenen to borrow
  1. First read

    You tap lenen in a story about books. Lectorist translates it and saves it to your list.

  2. A later read

    You see lenen in a story about bikes. This time you understand it without tapping.

  3. Reading review

    Lectorist brings the word up again. You can confirm you remembered it or say you’re still learning it.
